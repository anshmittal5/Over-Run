## Shockwave Evolution

Instead of a cooldown bar, the player can visually see the weapon charging.

Stage 1
- Blue lightning
- Small radius

Stage 2
- Brighter blue
- Faster lightning
- Medium radius
- Increased damage

Stage 3
- White/blue energy
- Large lightning arcs
- Massive radius
- High damage

The player can choose to fire early or wait for a stronger attack.
